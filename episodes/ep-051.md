Amanda founded [Just Mands Blog](https://justmands.com/) in 2015 and [Just Mands The Podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/just-mands-the-podcast/id1504125954) March of 2020. She is a 22 year old writer, thinker, learner, and sharer aiming to inspire others to do what they love and be who they are without fear. Amanda adores honest conversation, healthy living, simple beauty, womanhood, travel, real food, & fashion. As a blogger and podcaster, she uses her platforms to encourage self growth through uncensored banter and shameless oversharing.

Listen to me on Just Mands The Podcast: [https://apple.co/3p7M0nX](https://apple.co/3p7M0nX)

Follow Amanda on Instagram: [@justmands](https://www.instagram.com/justmands/) and[ @justmandspod](https://www.instagram.com/justmandspod/)

### Amanda and I dive into the following topics...

+ Amanda's background and what brought her to blogging and social media

+ Journaling and Amanda's tips to successfully getting out of your own head

+ The impact of social media on mental health and cultivating awareness around this

+ Navigating a surprise pregnancy at 22, a social media presence, and family relationships

+ Putting yourself out there and embracing authenticity

+ How to break into the blogging world
