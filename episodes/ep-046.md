This week I am joined by Evonna Kuehner, the CEO and founder of [ANOVÉ Beauty](https://anove.com/) ([@anovebeauty](https://www.instagram.com/anovebeauty/) on Instagram.) Evonna founded ANOVÉ® with the intent to create luxury skincare products for all skin tones and types. Noticing the lack of diversity amongst clean beauty brands and brand founders, Evonna set out to combine her background in Product Development with her passions for innovative ingredients and formulas and empowering others to look and feel their best. ([bio via anove.com](https://anove.com/pages/our-story))

### In this episode, we dive into...

> Evonna's transition from corporate life to starting her own buisiness from the ground up

> The CORRECT order you should be applying your skincare products

> Evonna's top 5 beauty/skincare tips

> Myths in the beauty world

> Creating balance between work and personal life and Evonna's experience as a new mother and the founder of a start-up

> Being compassionate and gentle with yourself during your wellness journey

> So much more!
