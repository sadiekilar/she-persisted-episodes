*TRIGGER WARNING FOR SUICIDAL IDEATION + SELF-HARM*

Today, I am joined by [Sage Lally](https://www.instagram.com/sagelally/), the founder of [be](https://www.bewomn.com/). be. is a newsletter & community working to empower women and non-binary people to step into their collective experience and share what makes theirs different and the same. Find them on Instagram at [@be.womn](https://www.instagram.com/be.womn/), listen to [the be. podcast](https://open.spotify.com/show/1D6aNri68JAT4Qlw9NIdoJ?si=LiIztva1RnuKEtAjGh0zUQ), and subscribe to their weekly newsletter at [bewomn.com](https://www.bewomn.com/).

### Sage + I Dive Into the Following Topics...

→ Sage's mental health journey as a college student

→ Our experiences in DBT (Dialectical Behavioral Therapy)

→ Maladaptive coping mechanisms + why they're SO HARD to break

→ The importance of advocating for your emotional needs in the moment

→ All things be. (their newsletter, IG platform, storytelling approach, etc)

→ How to create a platform for others to share their stories

→ So much more!
