This week, I am joined by [Audrey Bailey](https://brushandbarley.com/about), the artist behind the hugely successful[Brush and Barley brand](https://www.etsy.com/shop/brushandbarley/). Her pieces surround her faith, the enneagram, and mental health. She's also a mother who has struggled with clinically diagnosed depression and anxiety since her teenage years. Audrey is also the host of [Painted Pieces](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/painted-pieces/id1500833969) which I was a guest on a few weeks ago! Listen to our episode [here](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/she-persisted-with-sadie-sutton/id1500833969?i=1000485572642)! You can find Audrey on Instagram at [@itsaudreybailey](https://www.instagram.com/itsaudreybailey/), [@brushandbarley](https://www.instagram.com/brushandbarley/), and [@paintedpiecespodcast](https://www.instagram.com/paintedpiecespodcast/).

**Topics covered in this week's episode...**

> Audrey's mental health and religious journey

> Being a lukewarm Christian

> Experiencing depression and anxiety as a teenager

> Toxic romantic relationships

> Post-Partum depression

> Mental health stigma in the Christian community

> The impact of social media on your mental health

> Supporting a loved one who's struggling
