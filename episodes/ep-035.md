This week's guest is Cassandra McClure, a multi-hyphenated entrepreneur in the beauty industry with a passion and commitment to clean beauty. She started out as a model and celebrity makeup artist, and now has her own patent-pending product, [Lash Binder™](https://www.lashbinder.com/)—a tool and method that makes strip eyelash application quicker, easier, and safer. As the host of the [Clean Beauty Podcast](https://open.spotify.com/show/1VQcns4HaNZUSqf2umUXLh?si=G1jcPqCLSkWGWK42FYS6ig), she highlights brands with a green and clean ethos and educates and raises awareness about ingredient safety in daily products. Cassandra’s current next move is [Clean Beauty Con](https://www.cleanbeautycon.com/register), a business summit that has gone virtual with the goal of supporting struggling business-owners in these unprecedented times.
