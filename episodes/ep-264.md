**if you know what you need to do to improve your mental health, but struggle to implement these changes into your life, then this episode is for YOU. **

today’s guest is [shireen rizvi](https://www.shireenrizvi.com/)— a licensed clinical psychologist who has conducted research and training in dialectical behavioral therapy (dbt) for over twenty-five years. in this episode, dr. rizvi and i discuss how to implement dbt skills into your daily life.

whether you’ve never heard of dbt before, or you’re a dbt expert, this episode isn’t just about learning the skills— it’s about figuring out how to actually *apply* them in your life. because while awareness is a crucial step in mental health recovery, it’s not the only step that matters.

we talk about:

• what to do if you struggle to trust your emotions

• safely navigating your most intense emotions

• figuring out your “life worth living goals”

• getting motivated to make positive changes in your life

• debunking common pushbacks against using mental health skills

• why dbt gives people more grace to make mistakes
