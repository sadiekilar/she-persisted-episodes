## playbook (Claude's read of the numbers)

## what travels
*(youtube shorts unless noted; baseline lift is 1.0. the data is youtube only.)*

- **short hooks, 6 words or fewer:** n=62, lift 0.94, vs 0.43 for medium (n=402) and 0.13 for long (n=107). "ANXIETY 101 | Ep. 120 SOLO EP" hit 2.37.
- **exactly one CAPS word:** n=198, lift 0.82, vs 0.28 with none (n=190). "how our CHILDHOOD affects our current emotions" hit 2.51.
- **"this is" hooks:** n=13, lift 1.24, 85% beat baseline. the top list has no example to quote, so treat the phrasing as a lead, not a rule.
- **topics:** social media (n=34, 1.05), sadie recommends (n=27, 1.02), troubled teen industry (n=28, 0.96), 20s (n=40, 0.93). boundaries also shows up in the top shorts: "A Therapist's Advice on BOUNDARIES" hit 2.43.
- **1–2 hashtags and no digits:** n=204, lift 1.0 and n=199, lift 1.0, vs 0.16 and 0.17 without. both are likely tangled up with the era the post came from and with episode numbers in titles, so don't over-read them.
- **full episodes:** guest episodes beat solo (n=138, 0.90 vs n=64, 0.63), and statements beat "you/your" titles (n=113, 0.90 vs n=65, 0.67). "198. Chrysalis School Montana: My Troubled Teen Industry Experience" hit 26x.

## what sinks
- **long hooks (12+ words):** n=107, lift 0.13, only 8% beat baseline. "WHY you should've advocating at SCHOOL | Ep. 87 feat. Sarah Hernholm" got 8 views.
- **3+ CAPS words:** n=58, lift 0.25 (n=36 at 4+, 0.24). "THIS 1 THING PREDICTS YOUR THERAPY SUCCESS?!" got 10 views.
- **topics:** trauma (n=29, 0.08), body image (n=34, 0.18), anxiety (n=47, 0.21) and self-improvement (n=110, 0.23) all lag on shorts.
- **"how to" hooks (n=33, 0.27) and "you/your" hooks (n=85, 0.33):** both are under baseline.

minis are too thin to read (28 posts, no feature with n≥8 beyond "you/your" at 1.07, about baseline).

## phrasings to reuse
- ___ 101
- how our ___ affects our ___
- a therapist's advice on ___
- red flags that you don't have enough ___
- this is ___
- ___'s advice for teens
- just keep going
- what if part of you doesn't want ___? (one-off winner, so thin)
- how to help someone who ___ (without making it worse) (episode title, one-off)

keep each to one CAPS word on the topic noun.

## how sadie edits
12 edits logged, all drafts changed, no performance data on them yet.
- **quotes become framings.** pulled lines like "nobody gets what a BATTLE it is" turn into "signs a teenager is STRUGGLING with their mental health".
- **hashtags are dropped every time**, even though hashtagged shorts did better in the data. check whether they're moving to the description before changing this.
- **CAPS moves to the topic noun** (STRUGGLING, PASSION, REALITY, FRIEND GROUPS) and stays at one word.
- **"how to", "signs" and "what ___ ACTUALLY" openers appear often**, and the posted hooks run longer (about 8–10 words), which cuts against the short-hook lift above.
