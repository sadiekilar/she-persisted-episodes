This week’s guest is [Scout Sobel](https://www.scoutsobel.com/), the host of [SCOUT Podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/scout/id1505252696) and cohost of [Okay Sis](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/okay-sis/id1434353917). She is not only the CEO of [Scout’s Agency](https://www.scoutsagency.com/), a PR agency that specializes in podcasts and women’s voices, brands, and companies but a huge mental health advocate and a bipolar survivor. If you want to hear more from Scout her podcasts' [Okay Sis](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/okay-sis/id1434353917) and [SCOUT](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/scout/id1505252696) are available on every listing platform and you can [follow her on Instagram at @scoutsobel](https://www.instagram.com/scoutsobel/).

**Resources mentioned in today’s episode…**

- For affordable online counseling and therapy go to [betterhealth.com/okaysis](http://betterhealth.com/okaysis)
