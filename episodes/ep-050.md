This week I am joined by Dr. Uma Naidoo. Michelin-starred chef David Bouley described [Dr. Uma Naidoo](https://www.instagram.com/drumanaidoo/?hl=en) as the world’s first “triple threat” in the food and medicine space: a Harvard trained psychiatrist, Professional Chef graduating with her culinary schools’ most coveted award, and a trained Nutrition Specialist. Her nexus of interests have found their niche in Nutritional Psychiatry.

Dr. Naidoo founded and directs the first hospital-based Nutritional Psychiatry Service in the United States. She is the Director of Nutritional and Lifestyle Psychiatry at Massachusetts General Hospital (MGH) & Director of Nutritional Psychiatry at MGH Academy while serving on the faculty at Harvard Medical School. (bio via [umanaidoomd.com](https://umanaidoomd.com/))

You can find Dr. Uma on Instagram at [@drumanaidoo](https://www.instagram.com/drumanaidoo/?hl=en), on her website at[ umanaidoomd.com,](https://umanaidoomd.com/) and order her book—Your Brain on Food—[here](https://www.amazon.com/This-Your-Brain-Food-Indispensable/dp/0316536822/ref=sr_1_2?keywords=your+brain+on+food&qid=1583859371&s=books&sr=1-2)!

### Dr. Uma and I dive into the following topics...

+ Dr. Uma's background and what led her down the path of nutritional psychiatry

+ How nutrition can truly impact our mental health

+ The gut-brain connection and their development

+ Foods to eat to improve depression, anxiety, chronic stress, and immunity
